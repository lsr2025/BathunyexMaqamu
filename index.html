SubjectAppointment of Bathunywe Business Enterprise as Strategic Implementing Agent; Authorisation of Letter of Authority, General Power of Attorney, and Service Level Agreement
1. Preamble

We, the undersigned, being all the duly appointed Trustees of the Azibuye Emasisweni MaQamu Community Trust (IT 1021/2007), in terms of the Trust Property Control Act 57 of 1988, hereby record this Written Resolution, which shall have the same force and effect as a resolution passed at a duly convened meeting of the Board of Trustees.

The Trust is the confirmed beneficiary of a full and final land restitution settlement under Court Order LCC 01/2009, granted by the Land Claims Court of South Africa on 13 June 2025, in terms of the Land Court Act 6 of 2023, relating to properties in Campedown, Mkhambathini Local Municipality, uMgungundlovu District Municipality, KwaZulu-Natal, at a settlement value of R16,743,000.00. The Trust acknowledges the presence of unlawful occupiers on portions of the Lion Park land and the urgent need for a structured, lawful, and constructive intervention.

2. Resolutions Unanimously Adopted

THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES HEREBY UNANIMOUSLY RESOLVES AS FOLLOWS:

2.1  Appointment of Strategic Implementing Agent

That Bathunywe Business Enterprise, represented by Ms Thembeka P Ndlovu-Nkabinde as Managing Director, be and is hereby formally appointed as the exclusive Strategic Implementing Agent and Consultant to the Trust with immediate effect from 6 April 2026, for the Lion Park Land Formalisation Programme, governed by the Service Level Agreement of even date, hereby approved in its entirety including Annexure A.

2.2  Authorisation of Letter of Authority (BBE/ACT/2026/LOA/001)

That the Trust authorises the issuance of a Letter of Authority in favour of Bathunywe Business Enterprise to represent and act on behalf of the Trust with all relevant stakeholders, including COGTA, DALRRD, RLCC-KZN, HDA, NHFC, DBSA, Land Bank, IDC, ABSA Bank Limited, Mkhambathini Local Municipality, uMgungundlovu District Municipality, the KZN Department of Human Settlements, private investors, legal practitioners, and all other parties necessary for the Programme.

2.3  Authorisation of General Power of Attorney (BBE/ACT/2026/POA/001)

That the Trust authorises the execution of a General Power of Attorney in favour of Bathunywe Business Enterprise, granting authority to sign applications to government and financial institutions; execute funding agreements subject to Trustee ratification above R500,000; establish and operate the Programme Revenue Account at ABSA Bank; issue Occupancy Certificates; engage professional service providers; and perform all acts incidental to the Programme within the limits specified in the POA document.

2.4  Programme Revenue Account

That the Trust authorises establishment of a dedicated Programme Revenue Account at ABSA Bank Limited, with Bathunywe as the authorised operating agent, for receipt and disbursement of all levy collections, registration fees, and programme grants per the Service Level Agreement waterfall.

2.5  Kwahlelwa Group — Technology Partner

That the Trust acknowledges and approves the involvement of Kwahlelwa Group, led by Ms Lonathemba Radebe, as the Technology Partner responsible for the LandLink Occupier Registration and Geospatial Intelligence Platform.

2.6  Limitation on Land Disposal

That nothing in this Resolution shall empower Bathunywe to alienate, donate, encumber, or otherwise dispose of any immovable property owned by the Trust without a separate specific resolution of the Board of Trustees.

3. Certification

We, the undersigned Trustees, certify that this Written Resolution has been passed unanimously and that we are duly authorised to execute it on behalf of the Trust.

3. Grant of Power

The Principal hereby appoints the Attorney as its true and lawful attorney and agent, with full power and authority to do all acts in connection with the Lion Park Land Formalisation Programme, including:

  • Commence litigation in the name of the Trust without prior written Trustee approval;
  • Sub-delegate this Power of Attorney without written Trustee consent.
5. Ratification and Duration

The Principal ratifies all acts lawfully performed by the Attorney within the scope of this Power of Attorney, and undertakes to indemnify the Attorney against all claims, liabilities, and costs reasonably incurred in good faith. 1. Parties

Kwahlelwa Group (Ms Lonathemba Radebe) is acknowledged as Technology Partner responsible for the LandLink platform.

2. Appointment and Scope

The Trust appoints Bathunywe as its exclusive Strategic Implementing Agent for the Lion Park Land Formalisation Programme with effect from 6 April 2026 for an initial term of 36 months, renewing automatically for 12-month periods. Scope includes funding mobilisation; stakeholder coordination; occupier registration and Occupancy Certificate programme; community engagement; township establishment technical oversight; Programme Revenue Account administration; and all programme management functions.

3. Programme Revenue Account

A dedicated Programme Revenue Account shall be established at ABSA Bank in the Trust’s name. All levies, registration fees, and grants shall be deposited and disbursed in this waterfall order: (1) ABSA bridging repayments; (2) Bathunywe levy administration fee — 8% auto-swept monthly; (3) Approved operational expenditure; (4) Trust operating reserve — minimum 10%; (5) Surplus to Trust development fund.

4. Termination and Governing Law

Either Party may terminate on 60 days’ written notice. The Trust may terminate immediately on unremedied material breach (20 business days’ cure period). On termination, all records and platform credentials transfer to the Trust within 10 business days. Governed by the laws of South Africa. Disputes — mediation first; if unresolved within 30 days, arbitration under AFSA rules, seated at Durban, KwaZulu-Natal.

1. The Parties

Party A (The Advisor): Bathunywe Business Enterprise (Pty) Ltd, a specialist land enablement advisory firm. Party B (The Trust): Azibuye Emasisweni Maqamu Community Property Trust (IT 1021/2007), the confirmed restitution beneficiary under Court Order LCC 01/2009 dated 13 June 2025, relating to properties in Campedown, Mkhambathini Local Municipality, at a settled value of R16,743,000.00.

2. Background & Recitals

WHEREAS the Trust is the registered legal entity of the Amaqamu Community, holding land rights in terms of the court order granted. WHEREAS the confirmed government land valuation for Phase 4 properties is R16,743,000.00. WHEREAS Bathunywe is a specialist land enablement advisory providing multidisciplinary services including planning, surveying, municipal liaison, investor origination, and development structuring. WHEREAS the Trust wishes to appoint Bathunywe as its exclusive Land Enablement Advisor and Investor Sourcing Mandatary for the properties. NOW THEREFORE the Parties agree as follows:

3. Appointment & Mandate

3.1 Appointment: The Trust hereby appoints Bathunywe Business Enterprise (Pty) Ltd as its exclusive Land Enablement Advisor and Investor Sourcing Mandatary for the Trust’s land holdings.

3.2 Scope of Mandate:

  • Town and regional planning advisory, including township establishment and rezoning applications
  • Land survey coordination and title deed management oversight
  • Municipal and regulatory liaison with Mkhambathini Local Municipality, uMgungundlovu District Municipality and relevant provincial departments
  • Stakeholder engagement and community facilitation on behalf of the Trust
  • Origination, sourcing and packaging of private sector developers and investors
  • Post-settlement development planning and business case preparation
  • Technology-enabled project tracking, reporting and documentation management
  • Any other land enablement services mutually agreed upon in writing by the Parties

3.3 Exclusivity: During the term of this Agreement, the Trust shall not appoint any other party to perform the same or substantially similar services.

5. Term & Termination

5.1 Initial Term: This Agreement shall commence on the date of signature and shall remain in force for an initial period of 24 (twenty-four) months. 5.2 Termination: Either Party may terminate on 3 (three) months’ written notice. Termination shall not affect any accrued rights or obligations. 5.3 Renewal: Upon expiry of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew on a month-to-month basis unless either Party provides 1 (one) month’s written notice of non-renewal.

6. Obligations of the Parties

6.1 Obligations of the Trust: (a) Provide timely access to all relevant documents, title deeds, survey diagrams, and court orders; (b) Facilitate introductions to community leaders, trustees and municipal representatives; (c) Attend stakeholder engagements; (d) Make timely payment of all fees; (e) Not circumvent Bathunywe by engaging directly with any developer or investor introduced by Bathunywe during the term and for 24 months thereafter.

6.2 Obligations of Bathunywe: (a) Perform all services diligently and professionally; (b) Maintain confidentiality; (c) Report to the Trust monthly; (d) Act with integrity; (e) Comply with all applicable laws.

7. Confidentiality & Intellectual Property

Each Party undertakes to keep confidential all proprietary or sensitive information received from the other Party. All work product, reports, plans, strategies, and deliverables produced by Bathunywe specifically for the Trust under this Agreement shall be the joint property of the Parties.

8. Dispute Resolution & Governing Law

In the event of any dispute, the Parties shall first attempt to resolve it by good faith negotiation. If unresolved within 30 days, the matter shall be referred to mediation, and if still unresolved, to arbitration under AFSA rules. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
